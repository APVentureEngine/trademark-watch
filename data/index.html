US trademark Gazette word marks — free weekly CSV

Every mark the USPTO published for opposition or registered, normalized to one flat schema, one CSV per weekly Official Gazette issue. 321,619 rows across 16 issues (2026-05-19 to 2026-09-01). No login, no rate limit, public domain.

Columns

serial, mark, owner, classes, event, status, filing_date, pub_date, transaction_date — event is published (published for opposition, the 30-day window opens on pub_date) or registered; classes is a space-separated list of international classes; serial links to USPTO TSDR.

How it is built

Fetched from the USPTO Trademark Official Gazette weekly XML, parsed and normalized by this open-source pipeline, republished after every Tuesday issue. Issue files are never rewritten, so a URL you cite today keeps returning the same bytes. Our matcher's precision on real §2(d) refusal pairs is published in benchmark/RESULTS.txt.
