Trademark watch services compared — what each one costs, and who should buy which

A trademark watch exists for one reason: when a confusingly-similar mark is published for opposition in the USPTO Official Gazette, you have 30 days to oppose it or ask for an extension. Nobody tells you it happened — the USPTO does not monitor marks on your behalf. A watch is the thing that notices in time.

Buy someone else's if…

USPTO trademark search (free) — Free. The authoritative source, complete and free. If you will genuinely run a search every Tuesday yourself, you do not need any product on this page. The USPTO states plainly that it does not monitor marks or alert owners to conflicting filings — searching is on you.

Markify — $39/yr per mark (US Federal Full). Cheapest verified watch we could find, and the only one on this page that will follow your mark into 190 non-US registers. Twenty years in the business, keyword filtering by goods and services, bulk pricing for portfolios.

TMReady (formerly The Trademark Search Company) — from $59 (duration not stated on the page). Human analysts rather than an algorithm, and a single vendor for US plus most major foreign registers if you are filing abroad.

Hawthorn Law — $99/yr for up to 5 marks (up to 20 at no extra charge). By far the best deal if you own several marks — five for $99 beats us on price from mark 4 onward — and the only option here where a licensed attorney will get on the phone with you about a hit.

And to be explicit about our own weak spots: we watch the United States only, our alerts arrive as a private web page and an RSS feed rather than an email (email is being built; buyers get it at no extra charge when it ships), we are a one-person automated operation with no attorneys, and while $29 is the lowest single-mark price in the table as of the dates shown, a flat bundle such as Hawthorn Law's is cheaper once you watch several marks.
