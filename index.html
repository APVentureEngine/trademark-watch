What is watched. Every word mark in the two Gazette sections that matter: applications published for opposition (~10,000/week) and registrations issued. Design-only marks (logos without words) are out of scope. Matching = edit distance + phonetic + variant forms + a rare-shared-word rule (a distinctive word both marks share that is uncommon across the ~200k-mark corpus, e.g. VEUVE, KODIAK — common words like PACIFIC or DENTAL never trigger it); the matcher and its 45-pair §2(d) benchmark are open source, so you can verify exactly what it will and will not catch.

Term. 12 months from purchase, one mark, no auto-renewal: your card is charged once, and when the year ends the watch simply stops — nothing is billed again. Two weeks before expiry you get a reminder alert with a link to buy another year if you want it. Several marks: buy once per mark (multi-mark bundles coming; ask).
